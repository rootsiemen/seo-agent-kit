# Local SEO Checklist

For businesses that win customers from a place: law firms, clinics, trades, stores. The order matters — profile first, citations second, reviews always.

## 1. Google Business Profile

- [ ] **Primary category** is the most specific one available (e.g. "Divorce lawyer", not "Lawyer"; "Plumber", not "Contractor"). Add every legitimate secondary category.
- [ ] Business name = the real-world name. No keyword stuffing ("Best Cheap Plumber NYC") — it's a suspension risk and it doesn't work anymore.
- [ ] Address, phone, hours, website — exactly correct. Mark service areas if the business goes to customers.
- [ ] **Services list** filled in, one entry per service, with descriptions. This is free keyword surface most profiles leave empty.
- [ ] Photos: storefront/office, team, work — real photos, refreshed occasionally, beat stock every time.
- [ ] Q&A: seed the 5 questions customers actually ask (cost, timing, areas served) and answer them yourself.
- [ ] Attributes (women-led, wheelchair accessible, payment types…) — all that apply.

## 2. NAP consistency & citations

NAP = Name, Address, Phone. Inconsistency across directories is a quiet ranking leak.

- [ ] Pick ONE canonical spelling of name/address/phone (down to "St." vs "Street") and use it everywhere.
- [ ] Core citations: Google, Bing Places, Apple Maps, Yelp, Facebook, the top directories for the country.
- [ ] **Niche directories — attorneys:** Avvo, Justia, FindLaw, Martindale-Hubbell, Lawyers.com, state bar listing. (Every profession has its equivalent set; being listed where the niche's customers verify credentials matters more than 50 generic directories.)
- [ ] Audit existing listings for old addresses/phone numbers from moves and rebrands — duplicates and stale entries are the usual finding.

## 3. Reviews flow

- [ ] A short review link/QR the business actually hands to happy customers at the moment of satisfaction (end of a case, end of a job) — not a mass email later.
- [ ] Every review gets a reply, good or bad, within a few days. Replies are read by the next customer, not the reviewer.
- [ ] Never buy reviews, never gate (asking only happy customers). Both are detectable and penalized.

## 4. Website tie-in

- [ ] One location/service page per real offering, linked from the profile (profile landing URL should be the location page, not the homepage, for multi-location businesses).
- [ ] `LocalBusiness` schema with matching NAP on those pages — see the [schema skill](../schema-gaps/SKILL.md).
- [ ] The site's directory/profile pages and the GBP must not contradict each other on hours, services, or areas.

## Reporting

Monthly, one page: profile views/calls/direction requests (from GBP insights), review count + average, citations fixed, AI visibility score ([BotView](../../tools/)) trend. If a number isn't in the report, it didn't happen.
