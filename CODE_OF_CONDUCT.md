# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a welcoming, harassment-free place for everyone, regardless of background, identity, or experience level.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive in issues, pull requests, and discussions.
- Critique the work, not the person.
- Assume good faith; most disagreements here are about SEO tactics, not people.
- Help newcomers — everyone ran their first audit once.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language of any kind.
- Spam, self-promotion, and affiliate-link dropping.
- Publishing anyone's private information.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or ban participants who violate this code. Report problems by opening an issue marked "conduct" or contacting the maintainer via the email on the GitHub profile.

This is a short-form conduct code in the spirit of the Contributor Covenant.
